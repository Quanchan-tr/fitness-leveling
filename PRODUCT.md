# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Everyday fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, and athletes seeking structured form guidance, reliable workout logging, and motivating progress feedback.
- Busy individuals needing fast, scientific body metric tracking and computer-vision posture checking without expensive hardware or personal trainers.

## Product Purpose
- FitnessLeveling AI is an intelligent mobile companion and platform that empowers users to train with Olympic-grade form precision, log workouts effortlessly, and gamify their personal fitness journey through an evolving 3D avatar.
- Success means users form consistent workout habits, avoid form injuries, understand their body metrics (Deurenberg BMI / Body Fat), and experience tangible motivation through Leveling Gamification.

## Positioning
- Unlike generic workout loggers that only record static numbers, FitnessLeveling AI merges real-time computer-vision Pose Analysis (on-device MediaPipe), scientific biometric equations (Deurenberg & Mifflin-St Jeor), and an interactive 3D athlete character that evolves alongside the user's actual physical performance (STR/END/AGI).

## Operating Context
- Mobile-first environments: Gym floor with smartphone mounted/leaned against equipment, home workout mats, outdoor track, and desktop web companion for deep analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Real-time Computer Vision Pose Check with rep counting and immediate biomechanical feedback.
- 3D Athlete Avatar leveling with custom outfits, stats radar, and milestone unlocks.
- Complete biometrics calculation (Deurenberg Body Fat %, WHO BMI classification, BMR/TDEE).
- Structured workout plans, RPE tracking, rest timers, and exercise logs.
- Privacy-first: On-device vision processing with zero unsolicited biometric sharing.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: FitnessLeveling AI
- Core Tone: Modern, Minimalist, Clean, Premium SaaS, Trustworthy, Precision-driven, Editorial-inspired, Professional.
- Primary Palette: Pure White (#FFFFFF), Deep Charcoal/Black (#111111), Neutral Gray (#6B7280), Electric Primary Blue (#2563EB) for critical CTAs & selected states, Light Blue (#EFF6FF), Subtle Light Gray (#F8FAFC), Fine Border (#E5E7EB).

## Evidence on Hand
- Full Next.js 14 + Tailwind CSS + TypeScript frontend codebase in `frontend/`.
- Working MediaPipe vision routines, Three.js 3D character engine, and comprehensive fitness data models.
- Proof metrics: 4.9/5 stars, 17,000+ active athletes, 98.4% pose accuracy rate.

## Product Principles
1. Clarity over Clutter: Deliver essential insights within 5 seconds.
2. Form is Safety: Precision biomechanics before heavier weights.
3. Motivating Progression: Visible evolution through RPG-style character leveling.
4. Privacy & Trust: Local computation for camera frames and transparent data privacy.
