# Design System: FitnessLeveling AI Mobile Landing Page

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design Direction & Principles
- **Aesthetic**: Modern, Minimalist, Clean, Premium SaaS / Mobile Product, Editorial-inspired.
- **Tone**: High whitespace, strong typography hierarchy, subtle interactions, professional & trustworthy.
- **Conversion Philosophy**: Clarity → Trust → Value Proposition → Social Proof → Conversion.

## Color System
- **Background Primary**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface / Subtle Light Gray**: `#F8FAFC`
- **Primary Text**: `#111111`
- **Secondary / Supporting Text**: `#6B7280`
- **Primary Blue (Accent/CTA)**: `#2563EB`
- **Blue Hover**: `#1D4ED8`
- **Light Blue (Badges/Highlights)**: `#EFF6FF`
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#E5E7EB`
- **Accent Emerald (Success/Verification)**: `#10B981`

*Rule*: Blue is reserved strictly for primary CTAs, active states, key highlighted phrases, and clickable elements. Avoid neon gradients or decorative noise.

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Modern sans-serif (`Inter`, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif).
- **Hero Display**: 64–80px desktop, 48–60px tablet, 38–44px mobile (font-weight 800, tight tracking -0.03em, tight line-height 1.08).
- **Section Headings**: 32–44px desktop, 28–34px tablet, 24–28px mobile (font-weight 700).
- **Body Text**: 16–18px, comfortable reading line-height (1.6), optimal measure (60–75ch).
- **Badges & Labels**: 11–13px, font-weight 600, uppercase tracking +0.05em.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Max Width Container**: `1280px` (with responsive padding: desktop 32px, tablet 24px, mobile 16px).
- **Scale**: Multiples of 4px and 8px (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128px).
- **Generous Vertical Breathing Room**: 80–120px between major sections.

## Component Specifications
1. **Navbar**: Sticky, clean white with frosted backdrop-blur, subtle border `#E5E7EB`, clear branding, desktop navigation links, and primary CTA. Responsive mobile sliding sheet navigation.
2. **Hero**: Contextual eyebrow tag, bold headline with single focused blue highlight, concise subhead, App Store & Google Play download badges, social proof cluster (stacked athlete avatars + 4.9 rating + 17M+ users / active athletes).
3. **Product Showcase & Mockup**: High-fidelity CSS/SVG vector smartphone and Apple Watch mockups rendering crisp, authentic fitness UI (real-time pose skeleton, live rep count, 3D avatar leveling, biometric radar).
4. **Trust / Media Bar**: Minimalist monochrome logos (TechCrunch, Forbes, Wired, Men's Health, Product Hunt) with low visual weight.
5. **Alternating Feature Rows**: 3 deep-dive features with category badge, value-centric heading, description, 3-4 checkmarked tangible benefits, and high-fidelity mobile app visuals.
6. **How It Works**: Clear 3-step numbered journey (01 Calibrate & Target -> 02 AI Real-Time Guidance -> 03 Evolve & Conquer).
7. **Social Proof & Testimonials**: 3 authentic, realistic user stories (Marathoner, Strength Athlete, Physical Therapist) with verified badges and rating stars.
8. **Pricing Plans**: Clean transparent tiers (Starter Free, Pro Athlete [Featured], Elite 1-on-1 Coaching) with annual discount toggle.
9. **FAQ Accordion**: 6 friction-removing questions covering hardware requirements, AI accuracy, privacy, offline mode, and free trial.
10. **Final CTA**: High-impact, clean conversion section with instant store buttons and QR code download option.
11. **Footer**: Minimalist 4-column architecture (Product, Company, Resources, Legal) + Copyright 2026.
